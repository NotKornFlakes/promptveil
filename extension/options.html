PromptVeil settings

Everything here stays on this device.

Lock with a password

There's no password recovery. PromptVeil never stores your password. If you forget it, the only way back is to delete your lists and start over.

Backup of your lists

The backup file is encrypted with the password you choose here (AES-256-GCM, key from PBKDF2-SHA256 with 600,000 iterations). It covers your "Always hide" and "Never hide" lists, not the placeholder vault.
